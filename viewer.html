MR Viewer

View any 3D model in mixed reality on your Quest.
Pick a file, then enter MR — it appears in your room and you control it with your hands.

🤏Pinch to place it (surface or mid-air)
🤟Pinch NEAR it to grab & move it around
🙌Two hands: move apart/together to zoom
✋One hand (away from it): up/down tilts, sideways spins
Supports GLB · glTF · STL · 3MF · OBJ · PLY. Enable Hand Tracking on your Quest and set the controllers down.
Pinch your fingers to place the model.
